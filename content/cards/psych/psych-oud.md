## At a Glance
- Offer medication to every patient with opioid use disorder. Methadone and buprenorphine reduce overdose and all-cause mortality. ^[asam-oud-2020, jama-moud-withdrawal-review-2026]
- Declining counseling is not a reason to delay medication. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Hospital experts agree that starting buprenorphine or methadone in the hospital is appropriate. Starting naltrexone is uncertain. ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026]
- Withdrawal treatment alone is not treatment. See [[psych-opioid-withdrawal|Opioid Withdrawal]] for starting buprenorphine and methadone. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- A benzodiazepine or other sedative is not a reason to withhold methadone or buprenorphine. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Every patient treated for or with a history of opioid use disorder leaves with naloxone. ^[asam-oud-2020]

## Definition and Staging
- Diagnosis rests on at least 2 of the 11 DSM-5 criteria within a year. ^[cdc-oud-diagnosis-2024]
- Tolerance and withdrawal do not count in a patient taking opioids only under appropriate medical supervision. ^[cdc-oud-diagnosis-2024]

## Severity
:::scale Criteria met
- Mild: 2–3 criteria ^[cdc-oud-diagnosis-2024]
- Moderate: 4–5 criteria ^[cdc-oud-diagnosis-2024]
- Severe: 6 or more criteria ^[cdc-oud-diagnosis-2024]
:::

## Diagnosis
#### Assess
- Take a full history of opioids used, route, last use and past treatment. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Ask about alcohol, benzodiazepine, stimulant and other substance use. ^[common-practice]
- Treatment choice weighs the patient's preference, past treatment, current illness and setting. ^[asam-oud-2020]

#### Tests
- Initial labs: complete blood count, liver enzymes and tests for tuberculosis, hepatitis B and C and HIV. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Consider testing for sexually transmitted infections. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Drug testing at assessment and during treatment. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Pregnancy test in anyone who can become pregnant. ^[asam-oud-2020]

## Treatment
1. Choose a medication
   - Methadone and buprenorphine are recommended. Extended-release naltrexone prevents relapse once the patient is no longer physically dependent. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - Psychosocial treatment is offered alongside. Lack of it does not delay medication. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - There is no recommended time limit for medication treatment. ^[asam-oud-2020]

2. Buprenorphine
   - A standard start waits for objective withdrawal. See [[psych-opioid-withdrawal|Opioid Withdrawal]] for standard, low-dose and high-dose starts. ^[asam-oud-2020, label-suboxone-film-2026]

   {{dose:buprenorphine-sl-oud-maintenance}}

   - Fentanyl exposure may need higher doses, and hospital starts can improve retention. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - Avoid the buprenorphine and naloxone combination in severe liver impairment. It may not suit moderate impairment. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
   - Check liver tests before and during treatment. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]

3. Long-acting buprenorphine
   - Monthly or weekly injections start after one tolerated transmucosal buprenorphine dose, or in a patient already on buprenorphine. ^[label-sublocade-2026, label-brixadi-2026]
   - Only a healthcare provider gives them, and never IV. Watch for worsening withdrawal or sedation after the first injection. ^[label-sublocade-2026, label-brixadi-2026]
   - Not for moderate or severe liver impairment, because levels cannot be lowered quickly. ^[label-sublocade-2026, label-brixadi-2026]

4. Methadone
   - For opioid use disorder it is dispensed only by a certified opioid treatment program. A hospital may give it when the patient is admitted for another condition. ^[label-methadone-2026]
   - Do not start methadone maintenance in the hospital without a program to continue it. See [[psych-opioid-withdrawal|Opioid Withdrawal]] for hospital dosing. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
   - Titrate slowly. Peak respiratory depression comes later than the peak effect. ^[label-methadone-2026]
   - Methadone prolongs the QT interval. See [[cardio-qt-prolongation|QT Prolongation and Torsades]]. ^[label-methadone-2026]
   - Benzodiazepines and other sedatives add to respiratory depression and death. ^[label-methadone-2026]
   - Adding a CYP3A4, 2B6, 2C19, 2C9 or 2D6 inhibitor, or stopping an inducer, raises methadone levels and can cause fatal overdose. ^[label-methadone-2026]

5. Extended-release naltrexone
   - After short-acting opioids, wait at least 7 to 10 days opioid-free before the first injection. ^[label-vivitrol-2026]
   - After buprenorphine, 7 to 14 days usually pass before naltrexone. Buprenorphine or methadone can leave a risk of precipitated withdrawal for up to 2 weeks. ^[asam-oud-2020, label-vivitrol-2026]
   - Do not give it with opioid analgesics, current opioid dependence, acute withdrawal, a failed naloxone challenge or a positive urine opioid screen. ^[label-vivitrol-2026]
   - With fentanyl use, consider starting it only in a structured, medically managed inpatient setting. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - Oral naltrexone is not recommended except in limited circumstances. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - Tolerance falls, so overdose after a missed dose or after stopping can be fatal. ^[label-vivitrol-2026]
   - Stop it for symptoms or signs of acute hepatitis. ^[label-vivitrol-2026, label-naltrexone-tablets-2026]

6. Patients not responding
   - Titrate methadone to the patient's response. Switch medications if side effects are intolerable or goals are not met. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - Ongoing substance use may call for more support and a more intensive level of care. ^[asam-oud-2020]

7. Overdose prevention
   - Give naloxone to the patient and train the patient and family in using it. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - Tolerance falls after withdrawal, a missed dose or stopping medication, so overdose risk rises. ^[asam-oud-2020, label-vivitrol-2026]

## Monitor
- Drug testing during treatment checks adherence to prescribed medication and other substance use. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Liver tests before and during buprenorphine. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026, label-sublocade-2026]
- Sedation and respiratory rate during methadone starts and after each dose increase. ^[label-methadone-2026]

## Escalation
- Involve addiction medicine, when available, for hospital starts and switches of medication. ^[common-practice]
- Outpatient starts that fail may need medically managed inpatient initiation. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
- Pain on methadone or buprenorphine: treat pain and opioid use disorder together, and consider a higher dose or split dosing. See [[hosp-pain-management|Pain Management]]. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Pain on naltrexone: use non-opioid analgesics or regional anesthesia. Opioids given to overcome the block need close monitoring in hospital. ^[asam-oud-2020, label-vivitrol-2026]
- Obstetrics and addiction medicine together for any pregnant patient. ^[asam-oud-2020]

## More Information
### Special Populations: Pregnancy, lactation, liver
- Pregnancy: methadone or buprenorphine is recommended and starts as early as possible. ^[asam-oud-2020, acog-co-711-2017]
- Hospital admission for a methadone or buprenorphine start in pregnancy may be advisable, especially in the third trimester. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Do not use a naloxone challenge in pregnancy. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Naltrexone in pregnancy may be stopped if the patient and clinician agree the relapse risk is low. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Breastfeeding is encouraged for stable patients on methadone or buprenorphine. See [[psych-opioid-withdrawal|Opioid Withdrawal]] for infant monitoring. ^[asam-oud-2020, lactmed-methadone-2024, lactmed-buprenorphine-2024]
- Naltrexone passes into milk only in small amounts and is not a reason to stop breastfeeding. ^[lactmed-naltrexone-2023]
- Severe liver impairment: avoid buprenorphine with naloxone. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Moderate or severe liver impairment: avoid long-acting buprenorphine. ^[label-sublocade-2026, label-brixadi-2026]
- Cirrhosis raises oral naltrexone exposure several-fold. The injection needs no change in mild or moderate liver impairment. ^[label-naltrexone-tablets-2026, label-vivitrol-2026]

### After the Acute Phase: Discharge handoff
- Hand off medication, an appointment and a supply or bridge plan before discharge. ^[asam-oud-2020, common-practice]
- Tell a methadone program about every dose given in the hospital. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
- Provide naloxone to every patient treated for or with a history of opioid use disorder. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Stopping methadone raises overdose risk if opioid use returns. ^[asam-oud-2020]

## Pearls
- Medication treatment lowers mortality, so offer it at every admission. ^[jama-moud-withdrawal-review-2026]
- Never delay buprenorphine for counseling or because of a positive drug test. ^[asam-oud-2020, common-practice]
- Check the opioid-free interval before extended-release naltrexone. A premature injection precipitates severe withdrawal. ^[label-vivitrol-2026]
- Stopping any medication lowers tolerance, so pair every stop with naloxone. ^[asam-oud-2020]
