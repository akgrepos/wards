## At a Glance
- Treat with methadone or buprenorphine, not abrupt cessation. Both work better than alpha-2 agonists. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Score with the {{calc:cows}}. Give the first buprenorphine dose only once objective withdrawal is present, to avoid precipitated withdrawal. ^[wesson-cows-2003, label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Already on methadone or buprenorphine: confirm the dose with the prescriber or program and continue it. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
- Withdrawal treatment alone is not treatment of opioid use disorder. Start or continue maintenance medication. ^[asam-oud-2020, jama-moud-withdrawal-review-2026]
- Look for alcohol or sedative withdrawal in the same patient. Active use of those drugs may need a higher level of care. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Give naloxone at discharge. Tolerance falls quickly after withdrawal, so overdose risk rises. ^[asam-oud-2020]

## Severity
:::scale Withdrawal score
- Mild: 5–12 ^[wesson-cows-2003]
- Moderate: 13–24 ^[wesson-cows-2003]
- Moderately severe: 25–36 ^[wesson-cows-2003]
- Severe: above 36 ^[wesson-cows-2003]
:::

- The {{calc:cows}} is an 11-item scale that a clinician completes at the bedside. ^[wesson-cows-2003]
- It tracks severity and guides timing of buprenorphine. It does not diagnose opioid use disorder. ^[wesson-cows-2003, asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]

## Causes
- **Interrupted opioid supply:** [!most-common] home opioid, methadone or buprenorphine not ordered on admission, NPO status, or a transfer that skipped the dose. ^[common-practice]
- **Antagonist or partial agonist given to a dependent patient:** naloxone, naltrexone, buprenorphine or a mixed agonist-antagonist. ^[label-naloxone-2025, label-methadone-2026, label-suboxone-film-2026]
- **Drug interactions:** inducers of the liver enzymes that clear methadone can lower its level. ^[label-methadone-2026]
- **Mimics and coexisting syndromes:** [!cannot-miss] ^[common-practice]
  - Alcohol, benzodiazepine or other sedative withdrawal. See [[neuro-alcohol-withdrawal|Alcohol Withdrawal]]
  - Medetomidine in the drug supply, which has its own withdrawal syndrome ^[medetomidine-philadelphia-2026]
  - Sepsis, gastroenteritis, thyrotoxicosis and anticholinergic or sympathomimetic toxicity

## Presentation
- Symptoms are anxiety, insomnia, pain, nausea, vomiting and diarrhea. ^[jama-moud-withdrawal-review-2026]
- Signs include a fast resting pulse and dilated pupils, both scored on the {{calc:cows}}. ^[wesson-cows-2003]

:::onset Time after the last dose
- Short-acting opioids (heroin, morphine): 8–12 hours, peak 48–72 hours | Clears in 7–10 days ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Methadone: 24–72 hours, peak at 3–8 days | May persist for several weeks ^[asam-oud-2020]
:::

## Diagnosis
#### Assess
- Take a full history and examine for withdrawal signs. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Ask which opioid, route, last use, any fentanyl exposure, any methadone or buprenorphine program, and sedative or alcohol use. ^[common-practice]
- Score the {{calc:cows}} at baseline. Time since last use alone is unreliable. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- A {{calc:cows}} of 8 or more (at least mild withdrawal) is the trigger used before rapid high-dose buprenorphine. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]

#### Tests
- Liver tests before buprenorphine. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Potassium and magnesium before methadone or lofexidine. Correct low levels first. ^[label-methadone-2026, label-lucemyra-2026]
- ECG before methadone when QT risk factors are present: heart disease, syncope, QT-prolonging drugs, or cocaine or methamphetamine use. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
- {{calc:qtc}} 450 to 500 ms: weigh risks and benefits of methadone and correct risk factors. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
- Do not start methadone with a known {{calc:qtc}} above 500 ms. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
- ECG with lofexidine in heart failure, bradyarrhythmia, liver or kidney impairment, or with other QT-prolonging drugs. ^[label-lucemyra-2026]
- Urine drug screen, pregnancy test and infection screen as clinically indicated. ^[common-practice]

#### Avoid
- Timing the first buprenorphine dose by hours since last use alone. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Naloxone or naltrexone in a dependent patient who is not overdosing. ^[label-naloxone-2025]

## Treatment
1. Start an agonist the same day
   - Methadone or buprenorphine is recommended over abrupt cessation. Stopping abruptly risks strong cravings and relapse. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - Alpha-2 agonists are safe and effective, but methadone and buprenorphine reduce symptoms better. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - Ultra-rapid detoxification is not recommended. ^[asam-oud-2020]

2. Buprenorphine
   - Give the first dose when objective signs of moderate withdrawal appear, not less than 6 hours after the last opioid use. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
   - Giving it too early can precipitate withdrawal. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]

   {{dose:buprenorphine-sl-opioid-withdrawal}}

   - Patient dependent on methadone or another long-acting opioid: the label recommends buprenorphine alone on days 1 and 2. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
   - Switching from methadone: taper to a low methadone dose first and start buprenorphine only with objective withdrawal. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020, asam-oud-2020]
   - Wait at least 24 hours after the last methadone dose. Waiting 36 hours or more lowers the risk of precipitated withdrawal. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
   - Fentanyl exposure: in one prospective emergency department cohort, precipitated withdrawal occurred in under 1% after larger initial doses. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]

3. Other ways to start buprenorphine
   - Low-dose initiation: tiny buprenorphine doses build over several days while the full agonist continues. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - High-dose initiation: after withdrawal develops, buprenorphine is escalated rapidly in 1 or 2 initial doses. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - Hospital experts rated low-dose and high-dose initiation appropriate and traditional initiation uncertain. ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026]
   - They rated giving naloxone on purpose to bring on withdrawal, then rescuing with buprenorphine, inappropriate. ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026]
   - Evidence is limited. One retrospective cohort found similar inpatient outcomes with low-dose and standard starts. ^[ldi-buprenorphine-cohort-2026]

4. Precipitated withdrawal
   - Sudden objective withdrawal after a buprenorphine dose, usually a {{calc:cows}} rise of more than 5 points. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - First-line treatment is more buprenorphine, plus alpha-2 agonists and symptom medicines. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - Weigh sedation risk with more buprenorphine in older, acutely ill or already sedated patients and in chronic lung disease. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - Severe or refractory cases need close monitoring and addiction medicine. Options include more buprenorphine, a full agonist, ketamine or dexmedetomidine. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - Continue buprenorphine as usual once it resolves. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]

5. Methadone
   - Already on methadone treatment: confirm the dose, the last dose date and any take-home doses with the program, then continue it. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
   - Never give a reported dose from self-report alone. A full program dose can be lethal if tolerance has been lost. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
   - Program closed and the dose unconfirmed: give a limited dose for withdrawal, shown below. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
   - After more than 5 days without opioids, tolerance may be lost. Restart cautiously with addiction medicine. ^[label-methadone-2026, common-practice]
   - A hospital may give methadone to a patient admitted for another condition, and for up to 3 days while definitive care is sought. ^[label-methadone-2026]
   - Methadone for withdrawal in a patient not on treatment is done in an acute care setting or opioid treatment program. ^[asam-oud-2020]

   {{dose:methadone-po-opioid-withdrawal}}

   - Methadone prolongs the QT interval. See [[cardio-qt-prolongation|QT Prolongation and Torsades]]. ^[label-methadone-2026]
   - Benzodiazepines and other sedatives add to respiratory depression. ^[label-methadone-2026, asam-oud-2020]

6. Alpha-2 agonist
   - Lofexidine is approved for withdrawal symptoms in adults. ^[label-lucemyra-2026]

   {{dose:lofexidine-po-opioid-withdrawal}}

   - Clonidine is an off-label alternative. ^[asam-oud-2020]
   - Lofexidine and methadone both prolong the QT interval. ECG monitoring is recommended when they are used together. ^[label-lucemyra-2026]

7. Symptom medicines
   - Adjuncts include clonidine, hydroxyzine, acetaminophen and NSAIDs, and they can be given ahead of buprenorphine. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
   - Ibuprofen treats pain and ondansetron treats nausea. ^[jama-moud-withdrawal-review-2026]
   - Loperamide treats diarrhea. Avoid promethazine for nausea, since it potentiates opioids. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
   - Hospital experts rated clonidine, hydroxyzine, loperamide, gabapentin and muscle relaxants appropriate. ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026]
   - They rated benzodiazepines and ketamine appropriate with less agreement. Benzodiazepines add respiratory depression with opioids. ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026, label-methadone-2026]
   - Hospital experts rated antipsychotics and barbiturates inappropriate for withdrawal. ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026]
   - Pain from another cause: see [[hosp-pain-management|Pain Management]] for patients on buprenorphine or methadone. ^[common-practice]

8. Full-agonist opioids
   - Hospital experts rated full-agonist opioids appropriate for withdrawal in three settings: ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026]
     - During methadone initiation
     - As a bridge during buprenorphine initiation
     - For patients who decline treatment
   - Morphine milligram equivalents do not apply to medications for opioid use disorder. ^[asam-oud-2020]

## Monitor
- Reassess withdrawal about every 2 hours while titrating buprenorphine. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Reassess 2 to 4 hours after the first methadone dose. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Watch sedation and respiratory rate after each methadone dose, since peak respiratory depression comes late. ^[label-methadone-2026]
- Check blood pressure and pulse before each lofexidine dose. ^[label-lucemyra-2026]
- Follow potassium and magnesium, and repeat the ECG if methadone is raised or the {{calc:qtc}} is long. ^[label-methadone-2026, common-practice]

## Escalation
- Addiction medicine: any buprenorphine or methadone start, fentanyl exposure, precipitated withdrawal or complex pain with opioid use disorder. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023, common-practice]
- Obstetrics and addiction medicine together for any pregnant patient. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Alcohol or sedative withdrawal in the same patient: see [[neuro-alcohol-withdrawal|Alcohol Withdrawal]]. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Oversedation or respiratory depression: hold sedatives and give naloxone in small steps, since a large dose can precipitate severe withdrawal. ^[label-naloxone-2025]

## More Information
### Where Guidelines Differ: Starting buprenorphine
:::compare Standard against low-dose against high-dose
- Standard: wait for objective moderate withdrawal, at least 6 hours after the last opioid. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Low-dose: begin before withdrawal with tiny doses while the full agonist continues. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
- High-dose: wait for a {{calc:cows}} of at least 8, then escalate quickly. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
:::

### Special Populations: Pregnancy, lactation, liver
- Pregnancy: treat with methadone or buprenorphine rather than withdrawal management. Withdrawal carries high relapse rates and worse outcomes. ^[acog-co-711-2017, asam-oud-2020]
- Hospitalization may be advisable when starting methadone or buprenorphine in pregnancy, especially in the third trimester. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Do not use a naloxone challenge to test for opioid dependence in pregnancy. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Higher buprenorphine doses and more frequent dosing are often needed in pregnancy. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
- Methadone levels fall as pregnancy advances, so higher or split doses may be needed. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Labor: continue the usual methadone or buprenorphine dose and add pain relief. Avoid mixed agonist-antagonists such as butorphanol and nalbuphine. ^[acog-co-711-2017]
- Neonatal opioid withdrawal is expected and treatable. Untreated opioid addiction carries worse pregnancy outcomes. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Naloxone crosses the placenta and can precipitate withdrawal in the fetus. ^[label-naloxone-2025]
- Breastfeeding is encouraged for stable patients on methadone or buprenorphine who are not using other drugs. ^[asam-oud-2020, lactmed-methadone-2024, lactmed-buprenorphine-2024]
- Buprenorphine: milk levels and infant absorption are low, and it is acceptable while breastfeeding. Watch the infant for drowsiness and poor feeding. ^[lactmed-buprenorphine-2024]
- Methadone: monitor the infant for sedation and respiratory depression. Risk is higher if methadone is started postpartum or raised to a high dose. ^[label-methadone-2026, lactmed-methadone-2024]
- Wean gradually. Stopping breastfeeding abruptly can cause withdrawal in the infant. ^[lactmed-methadone-2024, lactmed-buprenorphine-2024]
- Lofexidine has not been established in pregnancy and there is no milk data. ^[label-lucemyra-2026]
- Liver disease: avoid the buprenorphine and naloxone combination in severe impairment and check liver tests. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]

### After the Acute Phase: Discharge handoff
- Withdrawal management alone is not recommended. Patients should know the risk of relapse and overdose. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Hand off maintenance medication, an appointment and a supply or bridge plan before discharge. ^[asam-oud-2020, common-practice]
- Methadone: tell the program about the admission, the discharge and every dose given, so dosing continues without a gap or a double dose. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
- Do not start methadone maintenance in the hospital without a confirmed program to continue it after discharge. ^[samhsa-tip63-2020]
- Provide naloxone to every patient treated for or with a history of opioid use disorder. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Hospital experts rated starting long-acting buprenorphine before discharge appropriate. ^[jno-hospital-moud-consensus-2026]
- Hospital initiation of buprenorphine can improve treatment retention. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]

## Pearls
- Time since last use is unreliable. Use signs and the {{calc:cows}}. ^[label-suboxone-film-2026]
- Detoxification without maintenance treatment loses tolerance and raises overdose risk. ^[asam-oud-2020]
- Check QT risk before methadone and lofexidine. ^[label-methadone-2026, label-lucemyra-2026]
- Fentanyl and other synthetic opioids complicate starting buprenorphine and methadone. ^[asam-hpso-buprenorphine-2023]
